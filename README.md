# UTPF Videos and Infographics

Short video overviews and infographics made with Google NotebookLM, about markets as a gas and the wider economy.

## Videos

- **The Market as a Gas** — a video overview of the market-as-gas idea.
- **UTPF: Markets as Weather** — a video overview of markets as weather.
- **重構過去：從麻將創傷到 AI 模擬器** (in Chinese) — from a mahjong story to an AI simulator.
- **F與G：人際溝通的物理學** (in Chinese) — free energy F and G as a way to understand communication: F is the inner burden, G is choosing a better outcome.
- **The Physics of Feelings** (in English) — the English version of the F and G communication story.
- **跨越五十七年的旅程** (in Chinese) — a 57-year journey, 1969–2026: from a classroom seed to the UTPF market simulator.
